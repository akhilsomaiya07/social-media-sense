Why 30m and 2h?

30 minutes: An APA research summary described a two-week study of U.S. college students asked to limit social media to 30 minutes per day; that was a study target, not a universal medical limit. View APA source.

2 hours: Social Media Sense uses 2 hours as a reflection point, not a medical threshold. The American Academy of Pediatrics says evidence does not support one universal screen-time limit for everyone and recommends looking at what media use displaces or disrupts. View AAP guidance.
