    How This Check-In Works

    Never means no interruption was reported for that question. Sometimes and Often describe occasional and frequent reported interruptions. This is a reflection on your answers, not a validated screening tool.

    Age, sleep, and activity provide context. They do not determine a diagnosis or a health score. Time alone is not used to label your habits.

    To choose one example to display, Often comes before Sometimes. Ties use bedtime, longer scrolling, responsibilities, physical activity, then time with other people. This display order cannot determine your personal priorities. Pre-bed use alone does not establish an interruption.
